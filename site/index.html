How this works · scope and limitations

A centered rolling minimum follows the lower envelope. An edge-aware moving average smooths it. Subtracting this estimate from the raw signal exposes peaks. This is a deterministic filter, not a learned model. Wide windows may erase broad peaks; a noisy minimum may sit below the true background. Endpoint forcing can introduce discontinuities.
